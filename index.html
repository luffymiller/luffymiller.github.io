The B2B Packaging Brand Growth Funnel

How structural product design converts cold B2B leads into high-LTV brand advocates.

01

Top of Funnel (TOFU) — Category Positioning & Authority

Capturing high-intent B2B traffic through technical packaging specifications, structural guidelines, and authoritative industry research. Establishing early trust before commercial intent matures.

02

Middle of Funnel (MOFU) — Unboxing Architecture & Custom Engineering

Demonstrating tangible value through custom sample kits, flute compression stress tests, and unboxing retention metrics that solve real transit damage and product presentation pain points.

03

Bottom of Funnel (BOFU) — Scalable Supply Chain Integration

Converting leads via seamless custom manufacturing quotes, tiered volume pricing, and automated packaging reordering frameworks engineered for long-term B2B retention.
